Privacy Policy

How Cyréna handles data and respects your privacy.

Overview

Cyréna is a static informational website. We do not collect personal data, use cookies for tracking, or run analytics scripts. This policy explains what little data may be involved and how it is handled.

What We Collect

We do not actively collect any personal information from visitors. The site does not use:

  • Analytics or tracking cookies
  • Advertising or remarketing scripts
  • Third-party embeds that collect data
  • Login or account systems

The only data that may be logged is standard server access logs (e.g., IP address, browser type, requested page) by the hosting provider. This is typical for any website and is not used for tracking or profiling.

Cookies

This website does not set cookies. If any cookies appear in your browser, they are likely set by your hosting provider or CDN for technical purposes (such as load balancing or security) and are not used to track you.

Third-Party Services

The Cyréna website does not load third-party scripts, fonts, or trackers. All assets (CSS, JavaScript, images) are served from the same domain to minimize external dependencies and protect your privacy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can reach out via info@vayanova.co.za.
