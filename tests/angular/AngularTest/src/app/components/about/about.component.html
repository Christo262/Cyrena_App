About Cyréna

Cyréna is a structured AI engineering agent designed to work with your codebase, not hallucinate around it. Built by developers, for developers.

What Makes Cyréna Different

While other AI assistants spray-generate code and hope for the best, Cyréna is an autonomous agent that:

  • 1. Understands Architecture — Respects project structure, naming conventions, and established patterns. No more fighting the framework.
  • 2. Builds and Fixes — Rolls up her sleeves, reads your code, understands constraints, and writes solutions that actually compile.
  • 3. Learns Your Patterns — Generates API references from your real code. Structured technical memory she reasons from, not theory.

The Platform Vision

Cyréna starts as an engineering agent, but with extensions she becomes a platform. Email integration. Calendar management. Custom workflows. Internal tools.

Your organization builds what it needs, distributes through your own servers, and your team gets a unified AI experience.
