Overview

Cyréna is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights. We believe in transparency and collecting only what is necessary to improve the experience for our visitors.

What We Collect

When you visit this website, we collect:

  • Analytics data — page views, visit duration, referral sources, and general browsing patterns
  • Technical data — browser type, operating system, screen resolution, and device category
  • Geographic data — country and city (derived from IP address, not stored)

We do not collect:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone)
  • Precise location data
  • Cross-site tracking data
  • Data from other websites you visit

How We Use Analytics

We use web analytics to understand how visitors interact with our documentation and which pages are most helpful. This helps us prioritize improvements and ensure our content is useful.

Specifically, we use analytics to:

  • Measure documentation page popularity and engagement
  • Identify broken links or confusing navigation paths
  • Understand which features visitors are most interested in
  • Improve site performance and content relevance

Analytics Provider: Cloudflare

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure site usage. Cloudflare is a privacy-first analytics provider that does not use cookies or persistent identifiers to track visitors.

Key privacy features of Cloudflare Web Analytics:

  • No cookies — Cloudflare does not set or read cookies on your device
  • No persistent identifiers — Visitors are not tracked across sessions or sites
  • Anonymized IP addresses — IP addresses are hashed and not stored in raw form
  • Aggregated data only — Metrics are summarized; individual visitor profiles are not created
  • No cross-site tracking — Data is not shared with advertisers or third parties

Because Cloudflare does not assign persistent identifiers or store raw IP addresses, we cannot identify individual visitors or associate analytics data with a specific person.

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We do not use first-party or third-party cookies for analytics, advertising, or tracking purposes. Cloudflare Web Analytics operates without cookies entirely.

We do not use third-party advertising cookies, social media trackers, or cross-site tracking pixels.

Data Retention

Analytics data is retained by Cloudflare for a maximum of 26 months, after which it is automatically deleted. Raw IP addresses are not stored — only anonymized geographic summaries.

Your Rights

Because Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies or persistent identifiers, and because IP addresses are anonymized, we cannot identify individual visitors or extract personal data from our analytics. The data we see is fully aggregated and anonymous.

What this means in practice:

  • Access / Deletion / Portability: We cannot fulfill requests to access, delete, or export "your" analytics data because there is no way to determine which data belongs to you. Cloudflare does not provide tools to identify or extract data for a specific individual.
  • Opt out: You can opt out of analytics tracking by using a browser that blocks analytics scripts (such as Brave, Firefox with Enhanced Tracking Protection, or Safari with Intelligent Tracking Prevention). You may also use a content blocker or DNS-level filter that blocks static.cloudflareinsights.com.

If you have questions about your privacy rights, contact us at info@vayanova.co.za.

Third-Party Services

Cloudflare processes analytics data on our behalf. Cloudflare is contractually bound to use data only for the purposes described in this policy and is prohibited from sharing or selling data. Cloudflare's privacy practices are governed by their Privacy Policy and Data Processing Addendum.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy as our analytics practices evolve. Significant changes will be noted with a revised "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
